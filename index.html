About Me

I work as the Director of Operations at a Montessori school in Maine. My work includes communication, organization, education, and creative problem-solving.

My Creative Background

Outside of work, I create colorful paintings and handmade goods through my small art business, Liza's Noggin. I enjoy illustration, painting, printmaking, jewelry making, and experimenting with different materials.

Art & Illustration

I enjoy creating colorful paintings, prints, stickers, and handmade artwork inspired by nature and everyday life.

Education

I enjoy supporting children, teachers, and families through my work at Pine Grove Montessori School. I love creating organized systems, designing resources, and helping build a welcoming community.

Web & Graphic Design

As a graduate student in web design, I enjoy learning HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and graphic design. My goal is to combine creativity and technology to create attractive, user-friendly websites and digital experiences.

Career Goals

My goal is to combine my experience in education, administration, and visual art with my graduate studies in web design. I hope to create engaging websites, branding, and digital resources that help schools, nonprofits, and small businesses communicate more effectively.

I enjoy solving problems through thoughtful design and look forward to continuing to build my skills in responsive web design, user experience, and graphic design.
